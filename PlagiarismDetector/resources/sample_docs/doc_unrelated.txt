Baking artisan sourdough bread requires patience, quality flour, fresh water, and active wild yeast cultures.
The fermentation process develops deep lactic and acetic organic acids that provide sourdough with its signature tangy flavor.
During proofing, gluten strands stretch and trap carbon dioxide gas bubbles produced by yeast respiration.
Bakers preheat heavy cast iron Dutch ovens to intense temperatures to simulate professional steam-injected hearth deck ovens.
A crisp golden crust crackles delightfully when cooling on an open wire rack in the kitchen.
Mastering hydration percentages and dough temperatures separates novice home bakers from master Parisian pastry chefs.
Freshly milled whole grain wheat offers rich nutrients and earthy aromas compared to industrial bleached white flour.
